WiiMart's Anniversary!

...is in like 11 days (March 12, 2026)

Thank you everyone, for making WiiMart last so long we're making it to one full year of public access!
But alas, niche projects like WiiMart are subject to motivation loss, funding issues, community decline, lack of technical knowledge, and other hurdles.

Recently, we've been acknowledging and discussing what the project has been through, if the project can survive due to these issues, and even if staff members are staying.
So on this anniversary, and as a coping last ditch effort, I'm going to gather what the community thinks about the project. Cue the form!

WiiMart 1 Year Anniversary Feedback

Here it is, this form has questions based on your experience with WiiMart and even if you are willing to help it out.
This wasn't a Q&A due to timezone issues. I will use this form to get issues answered by those who can handle it.

I get back to you on March 12 with the actions that staff may take, so it can be on the anniversary.
